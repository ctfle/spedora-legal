Your health comes first

  • Spedora gives no medical advice. Nothing in the app, on these pages or from the AI Coach is medical advice, and it must not be treated as such. Workouts, training zones, the FTP ramp test and the AI Coach's plans are general training information. They don't replace advice from a doctor or a qualified coach, and Spedora is not a medical device.
  • Talk to a doctor before you start training, or train harder than before, especially if you have a heart, circulation or breathing condition or another illness, are pregnant, are coming back from an illness or injury, or haven't done sport for a long time.
  • Stop riding at once if you feel unwell in any way, for example pain, pressure in your chest, dizziness or unusual shortness of breath, and get medical help if needed.
  • Choose intensity and duration yourself and listen to your body. In ERG mode the trainer sets the resistance; you can lower it, pause or stop at any time. Make sure your trainer and bike stand firmly, that you have enough space and fresh air around you, and keep water within reach so you stay hydrated.
  • On routes outdoors, traffic rules and your own judgement always come first.

Check that a plan makes sense for you before you ride it, and don't follow it if it doesn't. It doesn't know your health, and you shouldn't ask it for medical advice.
