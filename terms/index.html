Your health comes first

  • Spedora gives no medical advice. Workouts, training zones, the FTP ramp test and the AI Coach's plans are general training information. They don't replace advice from a doctor or a qualified coach, and Spedora is not a medical device.
  • Talk to a doctor before you start training, or train harder than before, especially if you have a heart, circulation or breathing condition or another illness, are pregnant, are coming back from an illness or injury, or haven't done sport for a long time.
  • Stop riding at once if you feel pain, pressure in your chest, dizziness, unusual shortness of breath or are otherwise unwell, and get medical help if needed.
  • Choose intensity and duration yourself and listen to your body. In ERG mode the trainer sets the resistance; you can lower it or stop at any time. Set up your trainer, bike and room safely.
